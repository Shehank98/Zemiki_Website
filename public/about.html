Welcome to Zemiki

Timeless design, honest craft

At Zemiki, every piece begins with a simple belief - that beautiful jewelry should be for everyone, every day. Our collections blend traditional Sri Lankan craftsmanship with modern design, creating pieces that feel both special and wearable.

From delicate everyday earrings to show-stopping bridal sets, each item is thoughtfully made and carefully finished. We're proud to offer flexible payment options - including KOKO, Mintpay and PayHere - so you can treasure your favourites today and pay your way.
