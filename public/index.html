How to Order

  2. 2CheckoutEnter your delivery details and pick how to pay.
  3. 3We confirm & dispatchYou get an email with your invoice and tracking link.
  4. 4Delivered to your doorPay on delivery if you chose cash on delivery.
How can I pay?
You can pay by bank transfer or cash on delivery, or place your order through WhatsApp. The options available are shown at checkout.
How much is delivery?
We deliver islandwide. The delivery fee depends on your district and is shown at checkout before you place your order.
How do I track my order?
After you order, we email you a personal tracking link. Open it any time to see whether your order is confirmed, packed, dispatched or delivered.
What if there is a problem with my order?
Contact us within 3 days of delivery and we will help. You can reach us on WhatsApp or through the Contact page.
