Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Zemiki ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website and place an order. By using our site you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, email address, and delivery address (including district and city) when you place an order or make an enquiry.
  • Order contents: the items you buy, amounts, and any gift message you add.
  • Newsletter: your email address if you subscribe to our updates.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends (such as IP address) used to keep the site secure and working. Your shopping cart is stored only in your own browser (local storage).

2. How we use your information

  • To process, deliver, and confirm your orders (including sending an invoice by email).
  • To contact you about your order via phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • To send offers and new-product updates if you have subscribed (you can opt out any time).
  • To protect the site against fraud and abuse.

3. Payments

Online payments are handled by our trusted third-party provider KOKO. When you pay online you are redirected to the provider, and your payment details are entered on their secure systems. We do not collect or store your full card details on our servers.

4. Sharing your information

We only share what is necessary to fulfil your order - for example, your delivery details with our courier, and payment information with the payment provider you choose. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

5. Email

Order invoices and newsletters are sent through a secure email service. Newsletter emails include an easy way to unsubscribe, or you can ask us to remove you at any time.

6. Data retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to provide our service and meet legal and accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your details where we are not required to keep them.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop receiving marketing emails. Contact us using the details below and we will help.

8. Cookies & local storage

We use a small amount of browser storage to remember your shopping cart and to keep the admin area secure. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
